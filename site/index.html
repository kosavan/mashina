Before you start.

How do I track car maintenance?

Create a vehicle, record service with its date and odometer reading, add parts and costs, and attach the receipt. Find previous repairs in the history and set a reminder for the next service.

When did I last change my oil?

Save each oil change as a service entry. Search the history for its recorded date, mileage, parts and cost. Add a date or mileage reminder using your vehicle’s service schedule.

Is it an alternative to vehicle maintenance apps?

Yes, if you want to run your own vehicle logbook. Track service history, mileage, fuel and expenses in a mobile-friendly interface. You manage installation, HTTPS and backups.

How does it compare with LubeLogger?

Both track maintenance and fuel on your own server under the MIT license. MASHINA uses React, FastAPI and SQLite, with a phone-oriented PWA and an isolated demo. LubeLogger offers Docker, a standalone Windows executable and a hosted demo. Compare their current documentation and workflows; this is not a claim of feature parity.

LubeLogger documentation

Can I try it without personal data?

After installation, choose “Explore the demo garage” on the welcome screen. The fictional garage has a separate temporary database and files. This website introduces the product; it is not a hosted instance of your garage.

Is MASHINA free and open source?

MASHINA’s own code is available under the MIT license. You provide the hosting. Third-party components retain their own licenses.
