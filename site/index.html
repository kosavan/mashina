BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

How do I track car maintenance?

Create a vehicle, record service with its date and odometer reading, add parts and costs, and attach the receipt. Use the history to find previous repairs and set a reminder for the next service.

How do I remember when I last changed my oil?

Record each oil change as a service entry. Search your vehicle’s history for it to see the recorded date, mileage, parts and cost. Add a date or mileage reminder based on your vehicle’s service schedule.

Is MASHINA an alternative to vehicle maintenance apps?

Yes, if you want to run your own vehicle logbook. It combines service history, mileage, fuel and expenses in a mobile-friendly interface. You manage installation, HTTPS and backups yourself.

How does it compare with LubeLogger?

Both track vehicle maintenance and fuel on your own server. MASHINA uses React, FastAPI and SQLite, with a phone-oriented PWA and an isolated demo inside your installation. LubeLogger offers Docker and a standalone Windows executable, plus a hosted demo. Both use the MIT license. Compare their current documentation and try their workflows; this is not a claim that either has every feature of the other.

Can I try it without adding personal data?

After installation, choose “Explore the demo garage” on the welcome screen. The fictional garage uses a separate temporary database and files. This website is a product introduction, not a hosted instance of your garage.

Is MASHINA free and open source?

MASHINA’s own code is available under the MIT license. You provide the hosting. Third-party components retain their own licenses.
