# Research and decisions

## Existing product and data
The fuel link navigated to general analytics and lost both selected vehicle and fuel filter; verified with a failing browser assertion before repair. Welcome art was a rectangular photo with unblended edges; use a composed local photo treatment and retain the synthetic demo disclosure.

Tire sets and events already use validated JSON in authenticated SQLite rows. Extend those documents additively; no table/revision is needed. Existing outbox supports tire_set, and authorization continues through the shared owner/driver gate. A backup round-trip verifies new fields without changing financial events. Research was delegated under speckit-plan to architecture_research; no infrastructure changes were proposed.

## Tire facts and uncertainty
DOT supplies a manufacture week/year, not an exact date or service start. Use the oldest tire's code explicitly, integer tenths for measured tread, stable measurement IDs and dated history. Mileage is never converted to tread/remaining life. A personal threshold is clearly distinguished from legal requirements and inspection.

[Michelin replacement guidance](https://www.michelinman.com/auto/auto-tips-and-advice/tire-buying-guide/when-do-i-need-new-tires), checked 2026-10-12: annual professional inspection after five years of service and preventive replacement after ten years from manufacture. Do not conflate these clocks. [Markings](https://www.michelinman.com/auto/auto-tips-and-advice/tires-101/tire-markings-explained) explain DOT. No US legal depth is presented as Russian law, no paid tire recommendation, no guessed weather.

## Service maps
Store address and coordinate pair locally. No geocoder, autocomplete, GPS tracking or new mandatory provider. Use the official OpenStreetMap embed only after a disclosed button action, scoped CSP frame-src and no-referrer on the parent iframe. Tile fetching happens inside OSM's own page; the app does not proxy, prefetch or offline-cache tiles. [OSM policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/tiles/) and [Nominatim policy](https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/) argue against an implicit public geocoding dependency. The card remains useful without a map.

## Fuel evidence
Payment screenshots rarely contain liters, mileage or full-tank evidence. Keep manual confirmation and exact two-of-three calculation, default station/fuel per vehicle, attach local evidence and link existing bank imports. Existing MCP is read-only; do not silently grant a model write access. Unfinished recognition code remains separate.

Read requested logo-design skill and Hyperframes core/CLI contracts. Created and inspected three black-first SVG concepts; owner explicitly selected Apex M and then green-only kit. Preserve graphite as a surface, no registered trademark symbol. Manrope outlines use existing SIL OFL font; fonttools is a development-only generator, not production dependency.

Hyperframes pinned 0.8.149, Node24 tool runtime, local GSAP/font/demo assets, telemetry disabled. The requested infographic is a static PNG snapshot with editable finite timeline, not an unrequested promotional video. Strict browser/layout/motion/contrast checks and visual inspection precede publication. Registry chart-story was inspected and rejected as unnecessary for a nonnumeric feature story. No cloud render or new app dependency.
