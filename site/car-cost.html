How to calculate the real cost of owning a car

At the end of the month, your fuel receipts tell only part of the story. Add maintenance and parking, then account separately for annual bills like insurance. Here is how to do it with one car and one month of fictional records.

Start with what you paid

This car travelled 900 km. Three kinds of expenses were paid during the month.

ExpensePaid this month
Fuel₽7,200
Service₽4,800
Parking and tolls₽1,600
Total paid₽13,600

Divide ₽13,600 by 900 km. That comes to ₽15.11 per kilometre for the month.

Budget for annual bills separately

If annual insurance costs ₽14,400, set aside ₽1,200 per month. Adding that allowance gives a monthly budget of ₽14,800, or ₽16.44 per kilometre at the same mileage. The allowance belongs in your budget. Keep the actual insurance payment in the month you paid it.

Use the same method every month

Log fill-ups with their date, volume, cost and confirmed odometer. Record service and other spending under their categories. Use confirmed odometer readings at the period boundaries. If a reading is missing, leave cost per kilometre blank until you have one.

In MASHINA, select the vehicle and period in analytics, then review spending by category. Keep purchase and sale figures separate when measuring running costs. If you want a full ownership figure for a car you have sold, add depreciation as a separate line: purchase price minus sale proceeds.
