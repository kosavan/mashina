# MASHINA identity exploration

Personal, private, precise, quietly premium. Existing equity: the name MASHINA and muted lime on graphite. The stock car icon has little distinctive identity; the replacement must work at 16 px and in one color.

Avoid: steering wheels, manufacturer badges, speedometer clipart, shields, racing stripes, checkered flags. Library study: travel/transport conventions plus openFrameworks, TensorFlow and IBM geometry; reference artwork is not copied or redistributed.

## Longlist and selection
Scores are clarity / distinction / simplicity / relevance / small-size, out of five.

1. Apex M — two journeys meet inside one personal garage; lettermark. 5/4/5/5/5.
2. Ownership orbit — an open history ring with an independent corner; abstract. 4/4/5/4/5.
3. Personal ledger — a protected spine and a floating event; abstract. 4/4/5/5/5.
4. Two-lane M with dashed center — road metaphor, too many details. 4/2/3/4/3.
5. Garage roof with event dot — pictorial, too literal. 4/2/4/4/4.
6. Kilometer flag folded into M — letterform, crowded junction. 3/4/3/4/3.
7. Wheel with a missing sector — emblem, category cliché. 4/2/4/4/4.
8. Stacked ownership chapters — combination, weak app-icon silhouette. 3/3/4/4/3.
9. Monospace MASHINA with one cut A — wordmark, no strong small icon. 4/4/4/4/2.
10. Keyhole between two lanes — privacy pictogram, too much security emphasis. 4/3/4/3/4.

The three selected forms use filled geometry on a 256-unit canvas. Apex: 40-unit stems, 45-degree diagonals. Orbit: concentric 100/64 radii with a separate square corner. Ledger: 40-unit spine and bars, 32-unit gaps. Refinement checks include 16/32/64 px, reversed, mirror and shelf comparison. These are concepts, not an approved identity or trademark clearance.
