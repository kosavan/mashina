# MASHINA Dusk identity exploration

Requested 2026-10-26 as part of the app, public site and repository Dusk refresh.
The user authorizes changing both mark and color. The MASHINA name remains.
The earlier green-only restriction and Apex M selection describe the previous
identity; they do not constrain this new exploration.

Audience: individual car owners maintaining a private, self-hosted vehicle history.
Adjectives: personal, calm, precise, tactile, quietly premium. The logo must work
as a 16px favicon, local PWA icon, app wordmark, GitHub header and public-site mark.
The interface uses local Manrope, slate/bone surfaces and periwinkle/apricot.

## Strategy

Retain the name and typographic clarity. Explore shape before effects, with a
single-color master. Periwinkle is the proposed primary identity color on slate;
dark slate on bone is the accessible daytime counterpart. No glass or gradient
is baked into the identifying geometry.

Avoid steering wheels, car silhouettes, speedometers, shields, checkered flags,
racing stripes and detailed roads. Library study informs construction and shelf
testing only; reference trademarks are not copied or distributed.

## Longlist

1. Fold M: a machined capital M with softened shoulders and a precise central fold.
2. Turn: two open turns forming a compact continuous history aperture.
3. Hinge M: rounded lowercase m with a lifted central bridge and an open passage.
4. Cut A: a custom wordmark with a single open A; too weak as a small app icon.
5. Archive loop: an interrupted annulus; too close to generic synchronization.
6. Horizon gate: a dusk arch with a baseline; risks reading as a literal garage.
7. Paired chapters: staggered rounded sheets; too close to a generic document app.
8. Quiet axis: two parallel rails with one turn; risks a pause/transport control.
9. Joined MA: linked initials; less immediate than the product's single M.
10. Kilometre fold: a folded marker; risks the familiar navigation-pin category.

The first three give distinct lettermark, abstract and soft-letterform readings.
Each is built in black and reviewed in 16/24/32px, monochrome and reversed views.
They are proposals, not a selected production identity. No trademark clearance
is claimed.
