# Dusk logo explorations B and C

Concept artwork only; production identity remains unchanged until the owner selects
a direction. Black first, transparent background, 256-unit symbol canvas.

## B: Turn

Two broad, opposing bends leave a 24-unit opening at each end of the track.
The history has a place to live and a way to continue. This is an abstract mark,
not a car or a literal road diagram. Its two unequal endpoints distinguish it
from a closed frame or ordinary chain link.

Construction: 40-unit filled bands, 208-unit square footprint, opposing 72-unit
outer / 32-unit inner turns. Version 2 relaxes circular tangencies into continuous
curves to avoid pinched joins. The visual centre remains exactly (128, 128).

Risk: its meaning needs the MASHINA wordmark at first; a viewer may read framing
or connection before vehicle history. The mark is deliberately neutral enough
to represent the whole private ownership history rather than a maintenance task.

## C: Hinge M

A custom rounded m has two quiet arches and a shortened central stem: independent
vehicle histories share one centre without closing the space beneath it.
The stepped centre is the engineered detail, visible as a 40-unit lift at all sizes.

Construction: 40-unit stems, 44-unit counter channels, 208-unit width, paired
smooth shoulder curves. Version 1 provides the ordinary full stem comparison;
version 2 lifts its centre by one stem width to give it a proprietary silhouette.

Risk: the friendly arches feel softer than automotive engineering; it is the
most approachable option, rather than the most assertive. It needs strict
spacing with the wordmark so it reads as a deliberate letterform.

## Research and tests

Reviewed the skill library's travel/transport conventions and elementary SVG
construction in Framer, Tidal, Kotlin and Pagekit. Reference marks were studied
for reduction and counter spacing; no reference paths were copied. Automotive
cliches (wheel, shield, speedometer, racing stripe) are excluded.

Both construction rounds passed `svg_audit.py` without findings (100/100 is the
script's heuristic, not a visual or originality guarantee). Reviewed the refined
symbols at 512 px, actual 16/24 px, and the preview sheet's size, monochrome,
reversed, blurred, mirror and rotated contexts. The 24-unit B apertures remain
open at 16 px; C's lifted centre remains distinguishable from its full-stem v1.
No production kit or deployment has been made for either candidate.
