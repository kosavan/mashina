BEFORE YOU START

A few useful answers.

How do I track car maintenance?

Create a vehicle, record service with its date and odometer reading, add parts and costs, and attach the receipt. Use the history to find previous repairs and set a reminder for the next service.

How do I remember when I last changed my oil?

Record each oil change as a service entry. Search your vehicle’s history for it to see the recorded date, mileage, parts and cost. Add a date or mileage reminder based on your vehicle’s service schedule.

Is MASHINA an alternative to vehicle maintenance apps?

Yes, if you want to run your own vehicle logbook. It combines service history, mileage, fuel and expenses in a mobile-friendly interface. You manage installation, HTTPS and backups yourself.

How does it compare with LubeLogger?

Both track vehicle maintenance and fuel on your own server. MASHINA uses React, FastAPI and SQLite, with a phone-oriented PWA and an isolated demo inside your installation. LubeLogger offers Docker and a standalone Windows executable, plus a hosted demo. Both use the MIT license. Compare their current documentation and try their workflows; this is not a claim that either has every feature of the other.

Can I try it without adding personal data?

After installation, choose “Explore the demo garage” on the welcome screen. The fictional garage uses a separate temporary database and files. This website is a product introduction, not a hosted instance of your garage.

Is MASHINA free and open source?

MASHINA’s own code is available under the MIT license. You provide the hosting. Third-party components retain their own licenses.

How to keep a vehicle logbook on your own server

Читать на русском

A useful logbook answers three questions. When was the last oil change? How much did the car cost this month? What needs doing next? MASHINA puts those answers in the same garage.

  1. Set up MASHINA. Use the pre-built Docker image on a home server, or use the standalone installation on Linux, macOS or Windows. Open the demo garage after setup to try the workflows with fictional records.
  2. Start with your next fill-up. Record the odometer, volume and cost. Mark a full tank when appropriate. MASHINA uses complete full-to-full cycles to calculate fuel consumption.
  3. Record your next service. Enter the date, mileage, work and cost. Attach the receipt so you can find it later, and set a reminder by date or mileage.
  4. Review costs at the end of the month. Filter by vehicle, period and category. Keep a complete backup of your records and attachments.

Как вести бортжурнал автомобиля на своём сервере

Read in English

Когда меняли масло? Сколько ушло на машину за месяц? Что пора обслужить? В MASHINA заправки, расходы и история ТО хранятся в одном гараже.

  1. Установите MASHINA. На домашнем сервере можно запустить готовый Docker-образ. Для Linux, macOS и Windows есть установка без Docker. После запуска откройте демогараж и попробуйте приложение на вымышленных записях.
  2. Запишите следующую заправку. Укажите пробег, объём топлива и сумму. Если залили полный бак, отметьте это в записи. MASHINA считает расход по завершённым циклам от полного бака до полного.
  3. Запишите следующее ТО. Укажите дату, пробег, работы и стоимость. Приложите чек и поставьте напоминание по дате или пробегу.
  4. Проверьте расходы за месяц. Выберите автомобиль, период и категорию в аналитике. Сделайте полную резервную копию записей и вложений.
