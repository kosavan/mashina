# MASHINA · Apex M

Selected by the owner on 12 October 2026. Two diagonals meet inside a strong M: one place for each vehicle's history. The symbol uses filled geometry, with 40-unit stems and 45-degree diagonals; no raster, filters or live text.

| Color | RGB / HEX | Approximate process CMYK |
|---|---|---|
| Chartreuse | 193, 238, 121 / `#C1EE79` | 19, 0, 49, 7 |
| Graphite | 16, 18, 17 / `#101211` | 11, 0, 6, 93 |

Screen RGB is authoritative; CMYK is an approximate conversion, not a matched print proof. No Pantone match is claimed. The owner requested **green-only artwork**: the final mark and wordmark use chartreuse, with graphite as the icon/background surface. Black/white concepts belong only to the exploration tests. Lime alone on white has insufficient contrast for small functional UI; place it on graphite.

Use at least 16 px for the symbol and 120 px for the horizontal lockup. Keep clear space of one stem width (40/256 of symbol width). Never stretch, outline, bevel, rotate or put the mark over busy vehicle photography. The small-size drawing retains the same silhouette. Maskable icons keep the entire mark inside the safe central region.

The wordmark uses outlined Manrope Bold with deliberate tracking. Manrope is SIL OFL licensed; [font license](../../backend/assets/fonts/OFL.txt). Masters can be regenerated with `python tools/build_brand.py` after installing the development-only `fonttools` package. No fonttools production dependency is introduced.

Deliverables: green symbol, outlined wordmark, horizontal/stacked masters; green favicon and iOS/PWA PNGs on graphite. Geometric audit and size/reversed screenshots were inspected. This is an original design exploration; trademark registration/clearance is not claimed. Do not append ®.
